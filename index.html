Most play buttons open an inline Spotify player right on the page — full tracks for Premium accounts signed in to Spotify in this browser, 30-second previews otherwise. “#” is a running chronological index across albums; “Track no.” is the position on that album. Patch is the update that added the content the track was written for; “1.x” marks version 1.0 additions whose exact patch isn’t documented. “Origin” notes the composer or whether a piece is new, arranged from an older Final Fantasy theme, or carried over from an earlier expansion.
